TinyLaunch positions itself as a friendlier, slower-paced alternative to Product Hunt: launches run weekly
rather than daily, and the top three products each week earn a TinyLaunch badge founders can embed on their
own site for social proof and a reciprocal high-authority backlink. Founded by indie maker Christopher
Woggon ("Chrissyinspace") in December 2024, it has built an audience of roughly 30,000 users, mostly
overlapping with the indie hacker and bootstrapped-SaaS crowd.
